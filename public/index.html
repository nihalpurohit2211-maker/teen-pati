How to Play Teen Patti

  1. Everyone pays the Boot amount (e.g. ₹50) into the pot at the start.
  2. Each player is dealt 3 cards face-down.
  3. You start as Blind — you haven't seen your cards yet.
  4. Players take turns going clockwise. On your turn you can: Peek at your cards, Chaal (call/bet), Raise, Fold, request a Sideshow, or call Show.
  5. The last player remaining — or the one who calls Show — wins the pot.
  6. Chips carry over between rounds. The game ends when players leave or go broke.

Blind vs Seen

🙈 Blind Player

  • You haven't looked at your cards
  • Pay the current bet amount each turn
  • Costs less — a valid strategy!
  • Cannot call Show on a Seen player
  • Cannot request a Sideshow

👀 Seen Player

  • You have looked at your cards
  • Pay double the current bet each turn
  • Can request a Sideshow
  • Can call Show when heads-up

Tip: Staying blind is cheaper but riskier. Peek when you need to make a big decision!

Sideshow and Show

Sideshow (Compromise)

  • A Seen player can request a Sideshow with the previous Seen player
  • Only when 3 or more players are still in the round
  • If accepted: both players compare hands privately — the loser folds
  • If declined: the game continues normally

Show

  • When only 2 players remain, either player can call Show
  • Both hands are revealed — the better hand wins the pot
  • A Seen player calling Show pays double the current bet as Show fee
  • A Blind player cannot call Show on a Seen player

Help & FAQ

Why did that player win? 🤔

Hands are ranked: Trail > Pure Sequence > Sequence > Color > Pair > High Card. If both players have the same hand type, the higher card values win. For example, a Pair of Kings beats a Pair of Tens. For ties in Color or High Card, each card is compared from highest to lowest — suit never matters in Teen Patti.

What is a Trail?

Three cards of the same rank — e.g. three Aces, three Kings. It's the strongest hand in Teen Patti. Three Aces (A-A-A) is the absolute best hand. Very rare!

What does "Blind" mean?

You haven't looked at your cards yet. Blind players pay less per turn (the current bet), while Seen players pay double. Staying blind is a legitimate bluffing strategy — but you can't sideshow or call Show while blind.

When can I Sideshow?

You must: (1) be a Seen player, (2) the player before you must also be Seen, and (3) at least 3 players must still be in the round. The target player can accept or decline.

What happens if I time out?

If you don't act within 35 seconds, you're automatically folded. Don't go AFK mid-game!

Can I fold even with good cards?

Yes! Folding is always an option. Sometimes it's better to cut your losses than keep betting into a pot you might not win. But if you have a Trail... maybe don't fold 😄

How are ties broken?

Cards are compared one at a time from highest to lowest. Suit NEVER matters for comparison in Teen Patti (unlike some Poker variants). A true tie (identical hand values) splits the pot evenly.

What is All-In?

If your chips drop to zero (or below the minimum bet), you go All-In. You stay in the round and can win the portion of the pot you contributed to, but you can't raise or bet further.

What is the Hand Strength bar?

The coloured bar under your cards shows how strong your current hand is compared to all possible hands. Green = strong, Yellow = moderate, Red = weak. It only shows to you — not to other players!
